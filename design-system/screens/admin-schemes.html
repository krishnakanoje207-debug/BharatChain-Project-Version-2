Add a scheme

Provisions its own on-chain ledger + ZK verifier

Compose this scheme's eligibility from any combination of attributes — income is one of many. Citizens prove they meet all criteria privately; the values are checked against the government registry, never disclosed.

What happens on save

BharatChain registers the scheme and provisions its own on-chain ledger — an approved-beneficiary set, approved-vendor set and spent-nullifiers keyed to this scheme — plus a dedicated ZK eligibility verifier compiled from the criteria above. The scheme stays Provisioning until the ledger & verifier are deployed, then flips to Active. Eligibility criteria lock once the first citizen enrols.
