How verification works

1
Sign up & apply
Create a vendor account and submit your business details for one category.
2
Admin verifies genuineness
A system administrator checks your business against the registry and approves or rejects.
3
ZK-enrolment
On approval you're enrolled on-chain for the schemes your category serves — privately, via a zero-knowledge proof.
4
Accept e-Rupee
Receive welfare payments, confirm-on-delivery, then convert tokens to ₹ — approved by RBI.

No pre-approved vendors — every merchant self-registers and is admin-approved. Your verified business identity is committed on-chain as a private commitment, never as public personal data.
