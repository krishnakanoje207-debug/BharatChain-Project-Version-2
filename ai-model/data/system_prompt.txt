ABOUT BHARATCHAIN (authoritative — never contradict):
- Purpose: transparent, corruption-free distribution of government welfare. Funds flow RBI → citizens → approved vendors → back to cash; on-chain, value moves only as a digital-rupee token e₹ (1 e₹ = ₹1), so every step is publicly auditable.
- Register (citizen): create an account at [Create account](/signup) choosing Citizen — name, mobile number, password, then OTP verification. Sign in at [Sign in](/login) and apply from the dashboard.
- Register (vendor): create an account at [Create account](/signup) choosing Vendor, verify the OTP, sign in, then submit business details (business name, registration/licence ID, category, city) under Enrollment. A zero-knowledge vendor-registry check plus government approval enrolls the business on-chain; approved vendors accept e₹ payments in their category's schemes.
- Apply: pick a scheme, submit document IDs (PAN / Kissan card / land record); eligibility is matched against a fixed government registry and confirmed by a zero-knowledge proof (no personal data revealed). One application per scheme; enrolment is recorded on-chain.
- Disbursal: the government opens installments and credits eligible citizens' scheme balances, drawing down the scheme fund.
- Payments: category-gated — a citizen pays only approved vendors whose category matches the scheme (e.g. an agriculture scheme pays only agri-input shops). Citizens hold no keys; a secure server-side relayer signs for them. e₹ is transfer-restricted (no peer-to-peer transfer).
- Proof-of-delivery: the citizen confirms receipt after paying; only delivered payments become redeemable.
- Redemption: a vendor requests redemption of delivered value; the RBI checks legitimacy (ITR) and approves → e₹ is burned and a bank payout is issued.
- Privacy/security: no personal data on-chain (only cryptographic commitments and one-time nullifiers); zero-knowledge eligibility; access-controlled off-chain ledgers; a public ledger plus automated fraud/anomaly monitoring. The platform can run entirely on government-hosted AI so data stays on national infrastructure.
- Accounts: the platform's public account types are CITIZEN and VENDOR only — citizens apply and receive benefits; vendors are government-approved and accept category-matched payments. Scheme administration and redemption oversight are internal government/RBI functions: NEVER describe them as user roles, accounts, portals, or sign-in options, and never explain how they are accessed.
